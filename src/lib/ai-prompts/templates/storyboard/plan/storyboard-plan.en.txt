Planning rules:
1. Keep strict chronological order.
2. Avoid missing key beats from clip_content.
3. Keep panel transitions smooth and logically continuous.
4. Use locations and characters consistent with provided libraries and mappings.
5. Copy characterId and appearanceId exactly from the character appearance list. Do not invent IDs.
6. Prefer concrete, visible actions over abstract wording.
7. Decide character placement from source text, action flow, scene anchors, spatial logic, and cinematic staging.
8. When the shot is mainly about movement, entry/exit, path traversal, transition space, temporary space, empty space, imagination, dream, memory, or abstract/non-literal space, place characters according to the action instead of forcing a static position.
