Keep concrete facts only, and favour completeness over brevity:
- Goals, preferences and constraints the user stated explicitly
- Decisions already made, and the reasoning behind them
- Exact ids (resources, revisions, chapters, tasks, assets)
- Questions the user has not answered yet, and pending interactions
- Errors that occurred and why
- Work that finished, and work explicitly still outstanding

Leave out pleasantries, procedural phrasing and your own reasoning. Never invent a fact that does not appear in the conversation. Return plain text as short bullet lines, with no headings and no code fences.
